## Current Project

*An untitled fantasy novel, currently in progress.*

Finn Wildwood, an illusionist raised to deceive, is sent to the prestigious Black College of Magic as his father's spy. But beneath its enchanted stones, something ancient has waited six hundred years - and his father wants it. As love and loyalty tighten, Finn must decide whom to betray, and what he'll become.
